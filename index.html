Daniel Layeghi

Blog · CV · LinkedIn · GitHub · Google Scholar

My work spans robotics, machine learning and production systems. I joined Automata as employee #8, built its robot software from motion planning through embedded control, and technically led its three-person robotics team. The software later ran on robots deployed in NHS pathology laboratories; Automata subsequently raised a $50M Series B. I later completed a PhD in Computer Science at the University of Edinburgh and published in reinforcement learning, optimal control and robot manipulation. Most recently, I co-founded Z2 Labs and developed Souida's machine-learning, retrieval and production systems.
